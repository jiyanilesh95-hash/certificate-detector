Certificate Detector - Test Images
==================================

You can test the Certificate Detector with these types of images:

1. GENUINE CERTIFICATE IMAGE
   - Save a real certificate or diploma
   - Take a photo of it
   - Upload to the website

2. ANY IMAGE
   - The detector will analyze:
     * Image quality
     * Text presence
     * Color distribution
     * Edge detection
   
3. WHERE TO GET TEST IMAGES
   - Your own certificates/diplomas
   - Screenshots of online certificates
   - Photos from the internet
   - Any image in JPG/PNG format

REQUIREMENTS
============
- Image format: JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, or BMP
- Maximum size: 10MB
- Minimum size: 100x100 pixels

TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
====================
1. Use clear, well-lit photos
2. Avoid blurry images
3. Capture entire certificate
4. Good resolution preferred

SUPPORTED FORMATS
=================
✓ JPG / JPEG
✓ PNG
✓ GIF
✓ BMP

HOW TO USE
==========
1. Prepare an image (certificate, diploma, or any image)
2. Open http://localhost:5000
3. Click upload area
4. Select your image
5. Click "Analyze Certificate"
6. View results instantly

ENJOY! 🎓
